# Repository Profile: SAGE-business

## Role
Public-facing company and business information for the SAGE product family.

## Publish here
- Company overview and public product catalogue
- Public roadmaps and partnership information approved for release
- Business model and market-facing documentation
- Public contact and support information

## Keep elsewhere
- Credentials, customer records, private contracts, internal financials, and unreleased security details
- Product source code and device-specific engineering artifacts

## Publishing standard
Every public claim about availability, partnerships, certifications, performance, or regulatory status must be verified before publication.
