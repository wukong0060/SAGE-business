# SAGE Business

SAGE is an independent technology company concept focused on connected software, gaming infrastructure, intelligent tools and platform services.

This repository is the business and company-planning home for the SAGE portfolio. Product source code belongs in dedicated repositories rather than being duplicated here.

## Portfolio

| Product / division | Purpose | Current engineering state |
|---|---|---|
| SAGE AI | Personal AI assistant and automation platform | Existing private repository: S.A.G.E |
| SAGE OS | Windows system management, diagnostics, customization and gaming optimization | Existing repository: SAGE-OS- |
| SAGE Droid | Android platform research for the Redmi 10A | Existing private repository: SAGE-Droid |
| SAGE FORGE | Brawlhalla player-development and replay-review workspace | Existing private repository: SAGE-Brawlhalla-Trainer |
| NEXUS | Cross-platform gaming storefront, identity, social and multiplayer ecosystem | Product specification / architecture phase |

| BBR-X | Large-scale expansion concept for Beach Buggy Racing | Creative direction and content planning |
| SAGE Telecom | Gaming-focused virtual telecommunications service concept | Business and technical roadmap phase |

## Operating principles

- Keep product repositories separate and clearly named.
- Publish honest implementation and validation status.
- Never present a concept, mock-up or source-validated package as a production release.
- Keep credentials, signing keys, customer data and private vendor assets out of public repositories.
- Use explicit permissions, privacy-aware telemetry and reversible system operations.
- Document third-party licenses and attribution before redistribution.
